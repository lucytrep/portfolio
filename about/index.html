Curious by nature.
Different by design.

Graphic and digital designer turned product thinker, currently completing my Master's in Communication Design at Parsons. My focus is digital product design, UI/UX, and AI-driven solutions that bridge user needs with business objectives.

I'm dyslexic, and it's given me a mind built for patterns and visuals. Reading and writing were never my strong suit growing up, but aesthetics, design, and a good challenge lit me up, and that's still what drives how I work today. I'd rather take the unfamiliar path than the safe one, which is why I've built products, taught myself new tools, and gone looking for the harder problem more than once. I'm a connector at heart, endlessly curious about what makes people tick, and that curiosity is what shapes every product I touch.
